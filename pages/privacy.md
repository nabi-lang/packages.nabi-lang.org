# Privacy notice

> **Draft.** This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet. It will change before the registry opens to the public.

This notice says what packages.nabi-lang.org keeps about you, why, and for how long. You can read packages without an account; nothing about you is kept then.

## What we keep

- **Your GitHub account**, when you log in: your numeric id, login, display name and avatar address, as GitHub gives them. They are refreshed at each login.
- **A session**, as a cookie that keeps you logged in for 30 days. It is only for logging in; we set no tracking or advertising cookies.
- **Publish tokens**, stored only as a hash.
- **What you do as a publisher**: which versions you published, yanked or unyanked, and when, and the owners and trusted publishers you added. This is part of the public record of each package and is shown on its page.
- **Moderation records**, when the admins act on an account or a package.

## Where it goes

- Pages and the API show your login, display name and avatar next to what you published.
- The registry runs behind Cloudflare, which carries the traffic and counts page views without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics).
- GitHub sees that you logged in here.
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

## Addresses and logs

The server keeps your IP address only in memory, for about a minute, to limit how many requests a client may send. Its logs hold the request path and status, not your address.

## How long

Your account stays while the registry runs, or until you ask us to delete it. The record of what you published stays with the packages, since other people depend on it; after a deletion it shows your GitHub id and login only.

## Your rights

You can ask what we keep about you, to correct it, or to delete your account: write to security@nabi-lang.org.
